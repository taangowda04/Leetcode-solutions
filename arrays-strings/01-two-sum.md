## Problem: Two Sum (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

### Approach

I used a brute-force approach by checking every possible pair of
elements in the array. If the sum of two elements matches the target,
their indices are returned.

### Complexity

- Time: O(n²)
- Space: O(1) excluding the returned array

### Notes

Tested the solution locally using a normal case and a duplicate-value
edge case before submitting it to LeetCode.
